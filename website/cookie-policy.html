Cookie Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how DJITT LTD (“DJITT”, “we”, “us” or “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies on our website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They can help websites function, remember preferences, understand website usage and, where permitted, support analytics or marketing activities. Similar technologies may also be used to store or access information on your device.

2. How We Use Cookies

DJITT may use cookies and similar technologies for purposes including:

  • Website functionality
  • Security
  • Remembering preferences
  • Understanding website usage
  • Website analytics
  • Improving our services
  • Supporting marketing activities where applicable

3. Necessary Cookies

Some cookies or similar technologies may be strictly necessary for the website or a service requested by the visitor to function. These technologies may not require consent where the applicable legal exemption applies.

4. Analytics Cookies

We may use analytics technologies to understand how visitors interact with our website. Analytics technologies that are not strictly necessary will only be activated where the required consent has been obtained.

5. Marketing Cookies

Where marketing or advertising technologies are used, these will only be activated where the required consent has been obtained.

6. Third-Party Technologies

Our website may contain services provided by third parties, such as:

  • Website hosting providers
  • Analytics providers
  • Scheduling platforms (e.g. Calendly)
  • CRM systems
  • Video or media providers
  • Payment providers
  • Marketing platforms

These services may use their own cookies or similar technologies. Where required, appropriate consent will be obtained before non-essential third-party technologies are activated.

7. Your Choices

Where consent is required, you can choose which categories of non-essential cookies you accept. You can also control cookies through your browser settings, although disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

8. Consent

Where consent is legally required, consent will be obtained through a clear positive action. Continuing to browse the website will not be treated as consent to non-essential cookies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when our use of cookies or similar technologies changes. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

If you have questions about our use of cookies, please contact DJITT using the contact details provided on our website.
