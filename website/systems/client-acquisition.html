Technology components

LinkedIn, Apollo, Instantly, Ulinc and GHL are part of the acquisition model, but they are not the core DJITT proposition. They are system components inside a broader operating architecture.

LinkedIn

Supports authority, visibility and trust-building in the market.

Apollo

Supports identifying and structuring target audiences with more accuracy and focus.

Instantly

Supports outreach sequencing and repeated contact where it is relevant to the system design.

Ulinc

Supports lead acquisition and conversation flow in the broader acquisition journey.

GHL

Supports qualification, pipeline, appointments and the handoff into the business operating system.

Why the architecture matters

01

Better targeting

Acquisition works best when the audience is defined with clarity and not just fed through volume-based outreach.

02

Faster qualification

Prospects move through a clearer experience with stronger qualification and less operational drag.

03

Better measurement

The business can see which parts of the ecosystem create quality conversations and which ones mostly waste time.
