How the route works

01

Business problem

Start with the real issue: weak visibility, fragmented operations, missed leads, or unclear conversion.

02

Insight

Clarify the pattern behind the problem so the visitor understands the root cause instead of a symptom.

03

Framework

Explain the business logic and operating model before introducing a solution, to stop the conversation from collapsing into tool-first language.

04

06

Provide the route to the right action, qualification flow or next conversation without forcing a generic sales pitch.

07

Next step

Once the issue is clear, the conversation moves to the most useful next step: a resource, a qualification conversation, or a practical system recommendation.
