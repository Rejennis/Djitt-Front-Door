DJITT Frameworks

Frameworks help businesses spot the gap between intention and execution.

The goal is not to add more complexity. It is to recognise the operational patterns that quietly limit growth, trust and service quality.

Core concepts

  • Judgement Gap
  • Architecture Gap
  • Digital Debt
  • Fidelity Ceiling
  • Resourcefulness Pivot
  • Productive Delusion
  • Contribution Call

Why the vocabulary matters

Judgement Gap

The space between what a business knows it needs and what it is actually able to act on consistently.

Architecture Gap

The mismatch between a business's goals and the structure of the systems supporting them.

Digital Debt

The compounding cost of disconnected processes, fragmented tech and operational complexity.

Recognise the pattern before trying to add more technology.

Every business problem has a system layer underneath it. DJITT works to make that layer visible.
