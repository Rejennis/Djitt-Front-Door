What breaks most acquisition systems

Good outreach, weak follow-up

Interest gets created, but momentum fades because the system is not designed to maintain trust and action.

Weak target definition

Businesses can put effort into a channel without really knowing whether they are reaching the right people.

No qualification architecture

Too much time is spent on low-fit conversations and not enough on the right business opportunities.
