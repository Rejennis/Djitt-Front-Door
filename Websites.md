One important change I'd make from the original DJITT copy

Your original WhatsApp copy says things like "sub-second page speed" and "built to be found". I'd be slightly more careful with absolute claims on the actual landing page unless you can consistently guarantee them. For example, "fast-loading" is safer than promising every site will load in under one second.

The strongest positioning for this offer is really:

You aren't selling "a website". You're selling a professional digital front door for a small business.

That gives the page a much stronger identity than simply saying "we build websites."
