# DJITT first content clusters

## Purpose

This file captures the first content clusters to develop after the content engine and public architecture are in place. These are the initial editorial themes that align with the DJITT front door, insights, frameworks and system positioning.

The goal is to create a portfolio of practical, business-led content that is anchored in real operating problems and then linked back into the appropriate system, resource and conversion route.

---

## 1. Intelligent Systems

### Working concept

This cluster explains what makes a business intelligently operational rather than merely automated.

### Topics

- What Is an Intelligent Business System?
- Automation vs Intelligent Systems
- Why Businesses Have an Architecture Problem
- The Hidden Cost of Digital Debt
- Why More Software Doesn’t Always Make a Business Better

### Positioning

Focus on system design, trust, workflow quality, operational clarity and how technology should serve the business rather than distract from it.

### Link targets

- Related system: business systems
- Related system: workflow operations
- Related system: AI systems
- Related resource: business systems assessment
- Related front-door route: not sure / business systems

- What Should a Financial Advisor Website Actually Do?

### Link targets

## Editorial rules

- Start with the business problem, not the technology.
- Use practical, specific language rather than broad buzzwords.
- Keep the content relevant to advisory firms and operationally mature businesses.
- Link each topic to the appropriate system, resource and front-door route.
- Avoid unsupported claims, inflated promises or financial-product positioning.

## Output expectation

The next stage is to turn these clusters into:

- individual insight pages
- resource pages
- route-linked system explanations
- internal linking between insight → system → resource → front door

This is the foundation for the public knowledge loop and the broader DJITT content engine.
